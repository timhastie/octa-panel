VIRTUAL PANEL …
The key matrix: events to the firmware are <row><column-bits> on the panel UART. Click a cell to tap that key (an encoder cell turns it +1) and watch the screen; to bind, click a panel key or knob first (it highlights), then the cell that drives it. Bindings persist in this browser.
COMPACT FLASH · AUDIO POOL — the card's AUDIO folder, what the unit's file browser sees. Add WAV/AIFF (16/24-bit 44.1 kHz; anything else — mp3, aac, flac, 48 kHz — is converted to 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV), then RE-INSERT CARD: the card image is rebuilt from the pool and the unit reboots (~40 s; the screen shows the phase). The same as copying files onto the card in USB disk mode and putting it back — the unit only sees files that are on the card it booted with.
or drop files on the card slot
HEAD-PHONES · MONITOR — the unit's main out (DSP core 0, L/R, 16-bit 44.1 kHz), drained by the server from the emulator into a ring of frames. Click the jack to plug the headphones in and listen; VOLUME is the monitor's gain (nothing of it reaches the firmware — the unit's pot is analogue, after the DAC). The emulator renders ~9x slower than real time while the sequencer plays, so listening is behind and comes in bursts (each burst waits for 300 ms of sound); a take — PLAY to STOP, what the unit played — replays at real time.
